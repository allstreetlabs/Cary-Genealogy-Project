# Rights and reuse

No blanket license is assigned in this foundation. Rights are retained by their respective holders. GitHub visibility alone is not a reusable license.

Before publishing a scan, photo, painting reproduction, transcript or long excerpt, establish origin, rights holder and permission/licensing basis. Being in family custody, depicting a deceased person or appearing in an old email does not by itself settle copyright.

The seed includes factual summaries and limited citations, not uploaded family images or full private correspondence. External URLs are references, not a sublicense to their content. Future media entries must record rights, credit, consent where relevant and publication decision. Select separate software and editorial-content licenses deliberately; do not apply one blanket license to third-party evidence.
