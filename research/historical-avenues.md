# Historical avenues — scope, not accepted descent

The research programme includes earlier Cary paternal generations, spouses' families, estates, public service and heraldry. Published genealogies offer leads through William Cary, Isabella Ann Shilson and George Rolle Cary. A continuous modern-to-medieval chain is not established in this public edition.

A proposed Courtenay/Bohun royal route depends on Philip Cary's maternity and all downstream links. The current HQ classification recognizes substantial published scholarly support for Margaret Courtenay's maternity, with a conflicting pedigree unresolved and original medieval evidence pending. The relevant History of Parliament attribution has been encountered through reproductions; its underlying references still need inspection. Do not present this as a verified royal descent.

Basset, Grenville and Paulet marriages provide additional avenues. Distinguish biological ancestry from a stepfamily marriage, collateral kinship, a cousin's title or court service. A separate Grenville–Courtenay parentage dispute must not be confused with Philip Cary's maternity.

The Aragonese-knight tournament story and traditional Adam de Kari origins require source criticism and early-record examination. A heraldic legend is not an established event or descent. Published reports of earlier seal evidence should be tested against the actual seal/transcript, not silently used to settle the story.

This page is a research agenda. Detailed candidate people and links are deliberately not inserted into the public accepted-tree dataset.
