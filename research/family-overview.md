# The recent Cary family: a working reconstruction

This public starter concerns Robert Cary, Alice Day and three reported children: Sir Robert Archibald Cary, Doris Winifred Cary and Gerald Andrew Cary. Ellen E. Cleminson is recorded as Gerald's reported wife. These relationships are currently family testimony or described records, not a completed documentary proof chain.

Gerald's reported registration is January–March 1914, Hampstead, mother's maiden surname Day, volume 1a, page 1082. The original full birth entry has not been examined by HQ. That index reference does not by itself identify both parents.

A privately held memorial reproduction names Doris Winifred Houston, née Cary, prints birth 20 August 1903 and “reborn” 24 December 1985. The latter is interpreted as a death commemoration, pending independent confirmation. The image is withheld from this public seed pending rights review.

Family testimony connects Gerald, Doris and Sir Robert. A reported omission from a recorded pedigree is a research question, not disproof. No inherited right to arms is asserted here. Documenting kinship and interpreting the limitation of a particular grant require separate evidence.
